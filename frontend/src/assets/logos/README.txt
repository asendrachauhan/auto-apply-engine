05 logo-vertical-light.svg             Light background centered branding
06 logo-mark-only.svg                  Generic mark where space is limited

15 logo-mark-light-background.svg      Mark on white/light backgrounds

Brand colors:

Recommended minimums:
- Favicon: 16–32 px
- Mark: 24 px+
- Horizontal logo: 140 px wide+

- App icon source: 512 px or larger
